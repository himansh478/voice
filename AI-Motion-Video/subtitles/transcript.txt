Kya tumhe bhi yahi lagta hai? Python language ka naam Python snake se relate karte hue rakha hai? But ye kahin bhi sahi nahi hai. Baat thi 1989 ki. Jahan par ek researcher Guido van Rossum ek aisi language chahta tha joki C se zyada fast ho, simple ho. Wahi usi time apni Christmas ki chuttiyon mein ek hobby project start karta hai. Jismein wo decide karta hai, Main ek aisi language par work karunga joki C se zyada fast ho aur efficient ho. Aur usne is language ka naam rakha ek British comedy show se relate karte hue joki tha, Monty Python's Flying Circus. Isi ke naam par usne apni language ka naam rakha Python. Ye Python language ek itna bada invention hai, joki machine learning, artificial intelligence, automation jaise sectors mein kaafi important role play karti hai. Socho, ek us purane time ka chhota sa invention, jiski wajah se, aaj kitna bada ecosystem ban chuka hai.
